# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project better:

- Assuming good faith, and asking before concluding.
- Giving and accepting feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Saying "I was wrong" without ceremony.
- Remembering that this is a teaching tool, and that people arriving to learn
  are the reason it exists. A question that seems basic is exactly the audience
  we are building for.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissiveness toward beginners.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private channel,
such as a direct message to a maintainer or a security-advisory style private
report. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and
the reporter's privacy will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
consequences determined by other maintainers.

Responses will be proportionate:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning.** Consequences stated for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban.** From interaction in project spaces for a stated period.
4. **Permanent ban.** From any public interaction with the project.
